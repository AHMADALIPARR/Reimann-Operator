\begin{abstract}
We present \textsc{Sovereign-RH-Lock}, a modular research architecture designed to investigate the Riemann Hypothesis (RH) through the Connes--Berry--Keating spectral program without conflating numerical coincidence with formal mathematical proof. The system enforces strict epistemic separation across six coupled layers: (1) high-temperature conjecture generation and bounded provenance-DAG branching via DSPy; (2) numerical spectral analysis, Von Neumann vs.\ Shannon entropy separation, Weyl perturbation testing, and 50-digit \texttt{mpmath} zeta evaluation; (3) data-parallel Structure-of-Arrays (SoA) pruning and compaction kernels in Futhark interfaced through a documented 64-bit little-endian Klong binary ABI; (4) open-world structural classification in OWL~2~DL; (5) closed-world admission, non-circular proof-dependency enforcement, and five-state epistemic tracking in Answer Set Programming (Clingo); and (6) a ten-gate verification controller. In sandbox validation, all 14 unit and integration tests pass, five nontrivial reference zeros are verified to $|\zeta(\rho)| \le 2.387 \times 10^{-50}$, and discrete Berry--Keating operators satisfy Hermiticity and Weyl stability bounds. Crucially, the architecture's proof-audit gate enforces that Gate~9 (exact spectral correspondence and completeness) and Gate~10 (RH proof admission) remain explicitly \textsc{Open} until all analytic obligations are formally discharged.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematical Objective and Proof Dependencies}

Let $\zeta(s) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n^{-s}$ for $\operatorname{Re}(s) > 1$, extended by meromorphic continuation to $\mathbb{C} \setminus \{1\}$, and let
\begin{equation}
Z_\zeta = \left\{ \rho \in \mathbb{C} : \zeta(\rho) = 0,\ 0 < \operatorname{Re}(\rho) < 1 \right\}
\end{equation}
denote its nontrivial zero set. The Riemann Hypothesis asserts that $\operatorname{Re}(\rho) = \frac{1}{2}$ for all $\rho \in Z_\zeta$. Following the Hilbert--P\'olya paradigm, \textsc{Sovereign-RH-Lock} investigates self-adjoint operators $D = D^*$ on an explicitly constructed Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}$ targeting the correspondence
\begin{equation}
Z_\zeta = \left\{ \frac{1}{2} + i\lambda : \lambda \in \sigma_{\mathrm{relevant}}(D) \right\}, \qquad \operatorname{ord}_{s = \frac{1}{2} + i\lambda}\zeta(s) = \dim\ker(D - \lambda I).
\end{equation}

Defining a symmetric expression or observing finite-dimensional Hermitian eigenvalues never constitutes a proof of RH. Every candidate correspondence in \textsc{Sovereign-RH-Lock} is gated on eight ordered analytic obligations (\texttt{OBL-01} through \texttt{OBL-08}) plus an inter-framework bridge (\texttt{OBL-BRIDGE-01}):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Explicit construction of $\mathcal{H}$ and proof of completeness (\texttt{OBL-01}).
    \item Definition of $D$ on a dense domain $\mathcal{D}(D) \subset \mathcal{H}$ (\texttt{OBL-02}).
    \item Proof of symmetry $D \subseteq D^*$ (\texttt{OBL-03}).
    \item Establishment of self-adjointness $\mathcal{D}(D) = \mathcal{D}(D^*)$ or valid extension (\texttt{OBL-04}).
    \item Exact trace-formula identity with the completed zeta function $\xi(s)$ (\texttt{OBL-05}).
    \item Analytic completeness: every $\rho \in Z_\zeta$ is represented (\texttt{OBL-06}).
    \item Accounting for multiplicities, continuous spectrum, and off-line resonances (\texttt{OBL-07}).
    \item Non-circular derivation of $\operatorname{Re}(\rho) = \frac{1}{2}$ (\texttt{OBL-08}).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Connes--Berry--Keating Operator Analysis}

On $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{BK}} = L^2(\mathbb{R}_+, dx)$, the Berry--Keating dilation operator $H_{\mathrm{BK}} = \frac{1}{2}(xp + px) = -i(x\frac{d}{dx} + \frac{1}{2})$ is unitarily equivalent via $u = \ln x$ to $-i\frac{d}{du}$ on $L^2(\mathbb{R}, du)$, which is essentially self-adjoint on $C_c^\infty((0,\infty))$ with deficiency indices $(0,0)$ and purely continuous spectrum $\sigma(H_{\mathrm{BK}}) = \mathbb{R}$~\cite{berry1999}. Restricting to a compact interval $[a,b] \subset (0,\infty)$ yields deficiency indices $(1,1)$ and a $U(1)$ family of self-adjoint extensions $b^{1/2}\psi(b) = e^{i\theta}a^{1/2}\psi(a)$ whose discrete eigenvalues $\lambda_k(\theta) = (2\pi k + \theta)/\ln(b/a)$ form uniform arithmetic progressions rather than GUE-distributed zeta zeros.

Conversely, Connes' adelic formulation~\cite{connes1999} acts by scaling on a weighted $L^2$ space over the idele class space $C_\mathbb{Q} = \mathbb{A}_\mathbb{Q}^\times / \mathbb{Q}^\times$, where nontrivial zeros appear as an \emph{absorption} spectrum in the trace formula rather than a direct emission spectrum. \textsc{Sovereign-RH-Lock} records the missing unitary/cohomological interface between these two frameworks as an explicit open obligation (\texttt{OBL-BRIDGE-01}).
